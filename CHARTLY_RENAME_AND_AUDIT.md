## 3. Race conditions — verdict

No new race condition was confirmed in production paths. Specifically verified
and **cleared** (initial suspects that inspection dismissed):

- The 9 bare `cachetools.TTLCache` sites: the two hottest
  (`services/strategy_module/webhook.py`, `broker/indmoney/...`) guard every
  access with a dedicated lock and fetch outside it; the other five also hold
  locks. Migrating them to `LockedTTLCache` is consistency, not a fix.
- `asyncio.run()` fan-outs in 4 brokers' `data.py`: correctly gated to the dev
  server via `utils.runtime`; production takes the thread-pool path.
- ZMQ bus: SUB-only bind holds (`websocket_proxy/server.py:131`); publishers
  `connect()` to the fixed `ZMQ_PORT` (`base_adapter._connect_to_zmq_bus`).
- `requests.*` calls carry timeouts (multi-line); exact-duplicate file scan is
  clean apart from intentional icon sizes; empty files are package markers;
  `broker/deltaexchange/mapping/transform_data.py` BOM is accepted by the
  tokenizer (cosmetic).
- The 1,100+ gthread concurrency tests pass (except the timeout in P9).

Not deep-audited (needs a live broker/feed): order-path idempotency under
concurrent webhooks beyond the existing tests, reconnect storms.
